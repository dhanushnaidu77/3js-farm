Building the farm…

Scatter feed for hungry hens, collect their eggs before they spoil and sell them on the farmhouse porch. Feed costs coins, and hungry hens stop laying. Save up for upgrades: more hen houses, a cow shed for milk, and a tractor to grow your own wheat. Catch the bus from the stop by the gate to Oakley, Millbrook and Saltby Bay — or explore the whole county on foot, by bike or by truck: deliver parcels, hunt for golden horseshoes, go fishing, ride the funfair and watch the sun set from Beacon Hill.
