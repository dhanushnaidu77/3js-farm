Run a little chicken farm, then explore the whole county of **Meadowshire**: three towns, rolling countryside, a funfair, a castle ruin, a lake and the sea. You can walk, cycle, drive or take the bus between them.

It's a single HTML file built with [three.js](https://threejs.org/). There's no install, no build step and no dependencies to download.

**Locally** download `index.html` and open it in a modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari). The game loads three.js from a CDN, so you need an internet connection the first time.

> If your browser blocks the module when you open the file directly, serve the folder instead:
> `python -m http.server` → http://localhost:8000

Your progress saves automatically in your browser's local storage.

## 🌾 What's in it

**The farm**
- Feed your hens, collect eggs and sell them at the market.
- Save up for upgrades: more hen houses, a cow shed, sheep, pigs, ducks, goats and a tractor with a wheat field to plough, sow and harvest.
- There's also a farm dog, a farm hand, and decorations for your yard.

**The towns**
- **Oakley:** a market town with a bakery, café, ice-cream parlour and pubs. It also has a park with a live cricket match, a bandstand, a playground and a whole residential street.
- **Millbrook:** a village with a church (you can ring the bells), thatched cottages, allotments, a duck pond, the village hall and a horse paddock.
- **Saltby Bay:** the seaside, with a pier, a funfair (big wheel, carousel, helter-skelter, coconut shy), a lifeboat station and rowing boats for hire.

**The countryside**
- Roads that link every town, plus dirt tracks across fields, woods and hedgerows.
- AI traffic drives the lanes.
- Landmarks to discover:
  - a windmill you can climb
  - a stone circle with a secret at night
  - a beacon on the hilltop
  - a lake with swans and a jetty
  - a ruined castle on the cliffs
  - Bluebell Wood
  - two neighbouring farms

**Things to do**
- 📖 **Villager storylines:** multi-chapter stories with reunions, a lost cat, a village wedding and a Best Kept Farm contest.
- 📦 Deliver parcels around the county.
- ✨ Find 24 golden horseshoes hidden around the county.
- 🎣 Fish from the pier or the jetty, then sell your catch.
- 🎯 Play darts in the pubs and the coconut shy at the fair.
- 🚕 Call a taxi from any red phone box.
- 🪑 Sit on a bench and watch the day go by.

**Visuals**
- A full day/night cycle with sunsets, stars, a moon, glowing street lamps and lit windows.
- Animated sea and lake water.
- A minimap and a full county map.
- Low, Medium and High detail settings.

## 🎮 Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `W` `A` `S` `D` | Walk / drive |
| `Shift` | Run |
| Mouse | Look around (click to capture the mouse) |
| `Click` / `F` | Scatter feed |
| `E` | Use / interact / get in and out of vehicles |
| `B` | Buy |
| `U` / `G` | Upgrades / sell wheat |
| `L` | Menus in town |
| `T` | Villager stories |
| `N` | County map |
| `R` | Activities (fishing, darts, coconut shy…) |
| `Q` | Eat a snack |
| `Space` | Jump / throw |
| `1`–`9` | Farm viewpoints |
| `M` / `H` | Mute / stats |
| `Esc` | Menu |
